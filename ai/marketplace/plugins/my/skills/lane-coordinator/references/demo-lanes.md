# Demo and recording lanes

- **Label what isn't real.** A scripted or planned flow says so on the first and last
  frame ("Concept preview: planned flow, not shipped") and marks each step shipped,
  in progress or planned.
- **Every claim maps to evidence.** On-screen labels and narration cite something the
  take or a dated report actually proves. If a phrase overstates it, the lane proposes
  different wording rather than quietly changing the meaning. Don't present one
  environment's footage as another's; a separate, titled segment from a dated real run is
  fine.
- **Real footage at captured speed.** No speed-ups inside model turns; extend cards, not
  footage. Fictional data and personas only.
- **Readable everywhere.** Keep text out of the bottom 15% (player controls) and top 5%;
  labels at least 40 px at 1080p; check frames with a simulated control bar and at 50%
  scale. Evidence panels: one fact at a time, held at least 3 s, solid background.
- **No invented events.** An event strip or log shows only captured evidence; if timing
  wasn't captured, reveal facts at observed state boundaries and say so.
- **Audit every frame** of each final file (credentials, local paths, usernames, emails),
  including stills.
- **Voice-over:** a cloud text-to-speech voice (labelled as AI in the notes, never a cloned
  or real person's voice); samples first for the user to pick; fit each beat's window by
  editing words or rate, never by speeding up video. Cloud resources follow
  [live-changes.md](live-changes.md).
- Keep v1 when making v2; write outputs under the project folder.
- Two to three minutes is the ceiling for a cut people will actually watch; start with
  the story and only the most interesting validation.
